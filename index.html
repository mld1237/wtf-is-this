Ecology & conservation

For native / non-invasive plants: Maine CoC runs 0–10; higher values mean stronger fidelity to high-quality, less-disturbed habitat. NatureServe S-ranks describe rarity in Maine and G-ranks describe rarity across the species’ full range. For invasives: use Maine’s official invasive rank and Maine distribution status; NatureServe I-Rank can be recorded when available.
